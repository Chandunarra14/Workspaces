Targets by role

Every role has a planned split of its target hours. The goal is always 80% of available hours. The role decides how that goal is divided between work areas.

Each row adds up to 100% of that role's target work. COMPed has no target for any role.

How it turns into hours

Chandler is in Field Operations: 40% Revenue Project and 60% Managed Services. Available 40 hours, goal 32. That plans 12.8 hours of Revenue Project and 19.2 of Managed Services. Admin is planned at 20% of available, 8 hours.

With PTO

The split applies to available hours after PTO.

With a PTO day, available is 32 and the goal is 25.6. Field Operations plans 10.2 Revenue Project and 15.4 Managed Services.

Where you see it

The gray Planned bars in Target mix, and the plan behind Mix Alignment. Target Attainment uses the flat 80% goal.

A role that is not in this table has no targets, so its target work shows as 0.

How time categories are set

Every hour logged in AutoTask is sorted into one work area. The rules are checked from top to bottom, and the first rule that fits decides. Nobody picks the category by hand.

Two words used below

Customer work means the task belongs to a customer account. DQ's own work means the task has no customer account, or the account is marked DQ Internal.

Project task means the task is part of a project. A task that is not part of a project is a ticket.

Revenue Project and COMPed use the same test with opposite results: revenue above zero, or none. The contract used is the one on the time entry itself, or the task's contract if the entry has none. Time that fits none of these rules is not counted as work and shows up as bench.

Walk through three entries

2 hours on a customer ticket, contract type Recurring Service.
Not PTO, not Scope Request. It is not DQ's own work. Rule 4 fits: Managed Services.
4 hours on a project task for a customer, project has revenue, not a Recurring Service contract.
Rule 5 fits: Revenue Project. With no revenue on the project it would be COMPed.
3 hours on a project task for a customer on a Recurring Service contract.
Rule 4 comes first and fits: Managed Services, even though it is project work.
1 hour of internal time with no ticket category and no project.
Rule 3 fits: DQ Admin.

Bench is not logged

Nobody logs bench. It is calculated: available hours minus everything logged. See how bench is calculated.

Time that fits none of the rules is not shown on any chart. Because it is not counted as work, those hours end up as bench. Each person is placed in their active, default department.

Why it matters

Project work on a Recurring Service contract is counted as Managed Services, not Revenue Project, because that rule is checked first.

A COMPed hour is real work, but no role has a COMPed target, so it never counts as target work.

Admin and Sustained Engineering are both DQ's own work. The difference is whether the task has a ticket category and a project, or neither.

What is left out before sorting

Time dated today or later. Data refreshes each morning with yesterday's entries, so the dashboard counts through yesterday only.

People in these departments are not in the dashboard: Account Operations Manager, Administration, Client Advisory Services, Client Success Manager, Executives, Network and Voice, Sales & Marketing and Support Center. A small list of named people is also left out.

The data starts on June 6, 2025. Scoring starts on September 1, 2026, so the Month, Quarter and Year pages do not count bench before that date.

How bench time is calculated

Bench is not entered by anyone. It is what is left of a person's available hours after PTO and all logged work are taken out.

The sum

Weekdays so far x 8 hours = 40
Minus PTO (0 here) = 40 available hours
25 target work5 non-target5 admin5 bench

Bench = available hours minus the hours actually logged. Sample numbers for one person.

Step by step

1. Count the weekdays so far, through yesterday, at 8 hours each. A finished week is 40.

2. Take off any PTO.

3. Take off every hour logged on work or admin.

4. What is left is bench. It never goes below zero.

Examples

Sam has five weekdays but took Friday as PTO. 5 x 8 = 40, minus 8 PTO = 32. He logged 28 hours. Bench is 4.
Someone logs 45 hours in a 40-hour week. Bench is 0, not negative.
Someone is on PTO all week. Bench is 0, not 40.

Good to know

Time logged to a task that fits no work area counts as bench, because it is not counted as work.

On the Month, Quarter and Year pages, time before September 1, 2026 is never counted as bench.

Today and future-dated entries are left out. Each weekday with nothing booked adds 8 hours of bench.

Read this guide in 60 seconds

The dashboard shows how people spent their working hours and whether that time went to the work they are expected to do. There is an Individual view and a Team view, each with Week, Month, Quarter and Year pages.

1

Numbers run through yesterday

Data refreshes every morning. Today's hours, and anything entered for a future date, are not counted.

2

A full week is 40 hours

That is 8 hours for each weekday. PTO comes off first. What is left is called available hours.

3

The goal is 80%

About 8 of every 10 available hours should go to target work. The other 2 are the admin allowance.

Week pages (WTD)

Sunday to Tuesday: last week

From Sunday to Tuesday the page shows last week's numbers, because the new week has little or no data yet. From Wednesday to Saturday it shows the current week.

Today is Tuesday, so you are reading last week, Monday to Friday, in full.

Month pages (MTD)

On or before the 5th: last month

Through the 5th of each month the page shows last month's numbers. From the 6th it switches to the current month.

Today is the 2nd, so MTD shows the whole of last month. On the 6th it starts the new month.

Mix Alignment

Whether the target work went to the right areas. 100% means every hour sat where the plan wanted it. Lower means some hours went to a different area than planned.

Versus share

Month and Quarter pages

These pages work like the Week page with a longer window. The cards read "of the month" or "of the quarter" instead of "of the week", and available hours count every weekday in the window through yesterday. The cards and charts are explained in the two chapters before this one.

Last month through the 5th, then the current month. The trend shows 6 months. The comparison chart shows this month, last month and the 3-month average.

Quarter

The current quarter so far. The trend shows 4 quarters. The comparison chart is named "Where the quarter went".

Same visuals as the Week page

How it is calculated

Attainment is target work divided by the goal. Status reads Exceeds at 105% or more, On target at 100% or more, otherwise Missed.

Chandler: 30 / 32 = 94%, so Missed target.

Glossary

Every term on the pages, in plain words.

Available hours
8 hours per weekday that has passed, minus PTO.
Goal
80% of available hours. For a full week, 32 hours.
Target work
Time on work the person's role has a target for. Admin, bench, PTO and COMPed are not included.
Non-target work
Real work that does not count toward the person's target.
Admin
Internal administrative time. Up to 20% of available hours is the allowance.
Bench
Available hours with no work booked.
PTO
Paid time off. It is taken out of available hours.
Target share
Target work divided by available hours.
Week example: Chandler has 40 available hours and logged 30 hours of target work. 30 divided by 40 = 75%.
Target attainment
Target work divided by the goal. 100% means the goal was reached.
Week example: the goal is 80% of 40 hours = 32 hours. Chandler logged 30 hours of target work. 30 divided by 32 = 94%.
Mix alignment
How closely the actual split of target work matches the planned split for the role. 100% is a perfect match.
Through yesterday
Today's hours and future-dated entries are left out, because data refreshes each morning.
Time category
The work area an hour is sorted into, such as Managed Services or Revenue Project. The rules are in How time categories are set.
Time that fits no rule
Not shown on any chart. It is not counted as work, so it ends up as bench.
Roster status
Exceeds target at 105% attainment or more, on target at 100% or more, otherwise missed target.
Why it may differ from AutoTask
This dashboard takes PTO out of available hours and stops at yesterday. AutoTask totals may include both.
